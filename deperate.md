# Excluded project ideas

These ideas are excluded from the initial shortlist for a solo founder or small team with a modest budget. Exclusion means less attractive as a first project under those assumptions, not that the idea cannot succeed with the right expertise, audience, data, or resources. Numbers match the original list. See [pick.md](pick.md) for the selections.
