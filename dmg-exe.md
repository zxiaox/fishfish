# 100 small desktop application ideas

Ideas for small, standalone applications that can be developed for both Windows and macOS. Each entry describes a focused first version. The first two are the examples provided; the remaining 98 are additional ideas. Validate demand and platform-specific distribution requirements before choosing what to build.

## Work and time

1. **Work Motivator** — Enter your pay rate and work schedule to see today's estimated earnings and time until clock-out. Support breaks and configurable currencies.
2. **Shift Countdown** — Show a live countdown to the end of a shift, with optional break and overtime indicators.
3. **Meeting Cost Meter** — Enter participant count, average hourly cost, and duration to estimate the cost of a meeting.
4. **Focus Sprint Timer** — Run customizable work and break intervals, with a daily summary of completed sessions.
5. **Time Zone Overlap Finder** — Enter several locations and working hours to find suitable meeting windows.
6. **Deadline Countdown Board** — Display a small list of deadlines with days, hours, and minutes remaining.
7. **Freelancer Time-and-Earnings Tracker** — Start a timer for a client or project and export hours and estimated earnings to CSV.
8. **Break Reminder** — Remind users to take a short break at adjustable intervals, with snooze and pause options.
9. **Workday Progress Bar** — Show a compact progress bar for the workday, accounting for scheduled breaks.
10. **Task Time Estimator** — Compare estimated and actual durations for tasks and show the user's average estimation error.
11. **Meeting Agenda Timer** — Divide a meeting into timed agenda items and alert the facilitator when each item ends.
12. **Overtime Calculator** — Calculate estimated regular and overtime pay from user-entered hours and rates.
13. **Shift Swap Planner** — Compare two people’s manually entered schedules to identify possible swap dates.
14. **Daily Accomplishment Log** — Record completed tasks throughout the day and copy or export a summary.
15. **Invoice Due-Date Tracker** — Track invoice dates and amounts locally and show which payments are approaching or overdue.

## Probability, math, and learning

16. **Monty Hall Problem Simulator** — Run individual or batch trials comparing switch and stay strategies; display the results and offer to email them to a specified address using the user's configured email method.
17. **Coin Flip Lab** — Simulate repeated coin flips and chart how the observed proportion changes as trials increase.
18. **Dice Probability Explorer** — Roll virtual dice, compare outcome frequencies, and view expected probabilities.
19. **Birthday Paradox Simulator** — Test how often a shared birthday occurs in groups of different sizes.
20. **Lottery Odds Calculator** — Enter a lottery's selection rules to calculate odds and explore simulated draws.
21. **Random Walk Visualizer** — Animate a one- or two-dimensional random walk and compare paths across runs.
22. **Compound Interest Playground** — Adjust contributions, rate, and duration to visualize an illustrative savings projection.
23. **Loan Repayment Explorer** — Enter a principal, interest rate, and term to compare estimated payment schedules.
24. **Fraction Visualizer** — Display fractions as shapes and illustrate addition, subtraction, and equivalence.
25. **Bayes' Theorem Explorer** — Adjust prior probability and test accuracy to see how evidence changes a probability.
26. **Sampling Error Demonstrator** — Draw samples from a simulated population and compare sample averages with the population average.
27. **Flashcard Trainer** — Create local decks, practice cards, and revisit missed answers.
28. **Typing Practice Timer** — Practice with built-in or pasted text and see speed and accuracy after each session.
29. **Vocabulary Recall Trainer** — Import word pairs and practice them in either direction with a simple score.
30. **Multiplication Drill** — Generate arithmetic exercises with adjustable difficulty and track practice results.

## Development notes

- Keep the first release focused on the core workflow; local storage and manual export can reduce complexity.
- Design for keyboard use, readable layouts, and different display sizes on both operating systems.
- Test each build on Windows and macOS; packaging, signing, notarization, and marketplace rules depend on the chosen distribution channels.
- For features involving email, notifications, or personal files, make the required configuration and data handling clear to users.
