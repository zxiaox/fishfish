# Feasible project picks

These are the stronger first projects for a solo founder or small team with basic writing, spreadsheet, design, or automation skills and a modest budget. Feasibility here means a small first version, low upfront cost, straightforward delivery, and an identifiable buyer—not a guarantee of demand. Numbers refer to the original 260 ideas. Narrow each offer to a specific buyer and workflow; review accuracy, permissions, and any sensitive information before delivery.

## Practical validation

1. Pick one buyer and interview a few potential customers about a recent instance of the problem.
2. Show a sample and test willingness to pay before expanding the product.
3. Deliver the first version manually or with a spreadsheet, document, or existing automation tool.
4. Improve it from customer feedback; build standalone software only when repeated demand justifies maintenance.
