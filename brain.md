A useful way to turn this list into a business is to combine **one buyer + one repeated problem + one deliverable**. For example, “a $29 template bundle for wedding photographers to handle client inquiries” or “a monthly reporting tool for small property managers.” AI can help create and maintain the product, but the thing customers pay for is the time saved or result achieved.
